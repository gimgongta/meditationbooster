## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Sit somewhere quiet and press play. A bell sounds for one minute.
Give it your full attention — and the moment the sound stops, meditation begins.

Meditation Booster is a deliberately simple app for anyone starting out.
Nothing to configure, nothing to choose. Just follow the four steps on the screen.

■ How to use it
1. Sit in a comfortable position in a quiet place.
2. Press the play button.
3. Close your eyes, take deep breaths, and focus your whole body and mind on the sound.
4. When the sound stops, your whole body and mind will be fully focused on yourself, and meditation begins.

■ Who it is for
· Anyone who wants to start meditating but doesn't know where to begin
· Anyone who prefers a light app with no subscriptions or extra features
· Anyone who needs a short pause before sleep or between tasks

■ Features
· No account and no sign-in required
· Shown in English or Korean, matched to your device language
· One minute — once a day is enough

■ Good to know
· An internet connection is required to load the content.
· This app is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a professional about health concerns.

Privacy policy: https://gimgongta.github.io/mb-privacy/
```
